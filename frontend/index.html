Connect to DMX4ALL interface

Connecting sends the DMX4ALL "C?" handshake and expects "G" back, so a wrong port is reported here instead of silently doing nothing. A failed connect falls back to the simulator; it never crashes the backend.

Raw channel test (bypasses fixtures/groups)

Hardcode one channel to confirm the wire is actually moving something -- e.g. set a moving head's dimmer channel to 255 and look for the beam.

Room Shape

Rooms are rarely perfect rectangles. Click empty space to place a new corner in order, or drag an existing corner to move it (any number of sides, any shape) -- the 3D view extrudes this outline up to the ceiling height below. Fixtures, objects, and safety zones move/rescale with the room when you save a changed shape, so they stay in the same relative spot. Leave it empty to keep a simple rectangle sized by width/depth.
